How the Learning Works

Every time you rate a claim, it's stored as a labeled example. Before each pipeline run, the AI receives your last 30 ratings as context — "Paul said this mattered, this didn't" — and calibrates its significance judgment to match your editorial taste.

After ~50 ratings the AI should reliably filter out irrelevant claims before they're fact-checked. After ~100 ratings you can consider turning off the feedback prompts entirely. The Recent Accuracy metric shows how well the AI's current significance predictions align with your most recent ratings.
